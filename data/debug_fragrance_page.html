Perfume rating 4.13 out of 5 with 53 votes

Check Please by Mind Games is a Oriental Woody fragrance for women and men. This is a new fragrance. Check Please was launched in 2025. Top notes are Green Apple, Nougat and Hazelnut; middle notes are Cinnamon, Plum and Bulgarian Rose; base notes are Amber, Bourbon Vanilla, Oak and Sandalwood.

Check Please paints a portrait of stylish magnetic charm with a character defined by a refined twist reminiscent of apple cognac. The fragrance opens with a refreshing burst of green apple balanced by sweet nutty hints of nougat and hazelnut. At its heart, the deep allure of black plum melts with warm cinnamon bark to create an air of intrigue and mystique. The composition is anchored by a rich foundation of oak barrel, while the creamy allure of vanilla, bourbon, and amber deepens the scent into a lasting elegant statement that celebrates bold, unforgettable experiences.

GourmandGoddess9410/28/25 02:33

I was sent samples for this and Playmate and I gotta say.. I’m absolutely mesmerized. I LOVE Double Attack but other than that I haven’t found anything from Mind Games that blew me away so thoroughly that I felt I needed a bottle right away but this sample from the first spray and sniff had me in love. I first experienced a love for how plum and green apple play together from Jest from Andrea Maack.. now they aren’t even close to each other because Jest is dark with chocolate and it’s heavily boozy while Check Please is far more feminine leaning and lighter. But the way green apple and plum play together here is reminiscent of Jest and I love it. The opening already had me with those two notes swirling together being so tart and juicy but then you get hints of cinnamon and hazelnut and a bunch of sweet nougat. It’s so good! This is the first Mind Games fragrance where I’m absolutely blown away in love and I know I have to get a full bottle there’s no other way around it but since I already got Lucid Dreams from Reinvented last week and Muse from YSL I knew a full bottle is gonna wait a bit but I did order a 9ml from scent split because I needed to have a bit of this to play around with and enjoy and wear just to make sure I’m as obsessed as the sample has me believing but I’m positive that I’m going to end up getting Check Please. I am a gourmand girlie and plum is one of my absolute favorite notes I mean.. Lucid Dream is also plum heavy and I am obsessed with that as well as Jest. Plum is a decadent note and fragrances that use it have been killing these compositions lately. I don’t think it’s blind buy safe but I do think it’s feminine leaning and if you know you love the notes in check please then there’s a good chance you’ll love how special and how damn good this is! But be sure to sample sample sample! I don’t know if I’ll review the other but just a disclaimer Playmate is too floral. I just don’t like florals very much but I will say I don’t think either offering leans masculine I think both lean feminine in my opinion buuuuuutttt I’d be more than okay with my man smelling like check please because it’s that yummy.

WhosHueyy10/25/25 16:38

This is a very interesting fragrance - just like the entire Mind Games scents you really have to let it dry out before rating it. It does slightly lean more feminine with the green apple - yet very very low you'll be able to pick up a nice scent of amber, but it's so slight that only a nose for fragrances would be able to pick it up. Personally I would rate the quality of ingredients and smell at a 8.5/10. With that being said, the smell itself, for me, is closer to a 7 or 7.5. Very beautiful but not really the best for men to wear. The type of men that would wear this would be someone who is wanting a lighter scent, wanting to smell good but not trying to turn any heads. Maybe a unique office smell or a driver for a summer. Could be a really nice base fragrance if you like to layer different ones together, but I'm not sure it's worth the price for me otherwise.
